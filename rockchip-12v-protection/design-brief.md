# Rockchip retrofit power protection board

**Revision A3 · Detailed schematic draft · 24 November 2026**

The existing 12 V, 5 A adapter feeds a protected, nominal **11.5 V rail** for a **3 A maximum Rockchip load**. The voltage at the Rockchip JST has a **12.0 V hard ceiling**. A3 expands the converter and independent output cutoff into pin-level circuits and supersedes the A1 and A2 drafts. Operation near 11.5 V remains a bench acceptance item.

```text
12 V adapter → fuse and TVS → reverse-polarity switch and input circuit breaker
             → 11.5 V buck-boost regulator → hardware overvoltage cutoff → Rockchip JST
```

## Input protection and power budget

- **F1 and D1.** The initial parts are a Littelfuse 5 A 451-series fuse and a 5.0SMDJ24CA bidirectional TVS, with the TVS loop returning to J1. D1’s published 38.9 V clamp at its datasheet pulse point leaves little margin to U1’s −40 V input protection rating on negative surges. Qualify both polarities at the agreed test waveform, source impedance, and temperature before releasing D1. F1 clearing a failed-short D1 depends on the source’s short-circuit mode and available current. [451-series fuse](https://www.littelfuse.com/assetdocs/fuse-451-and-453-datasheet?assetguid=533cd5cc-956c-4243-867f-6ab5a62f6ba1) · [5.0SMDJ TVS family](https://www.littelfuse.com/assetdocs/tvs-diodes-5-0smdj-datasheet?assetguid=2B2F4A8C-2E81-4894-8D41-3D748BB79E68)
- **U1, Q1, Q2, and RS1.** LTC4368HMS-2 with two PSMN4R8-100BSE MOSFETs uses a common-source, common-gate switch. R1–R3 give nominal **9.84 V falling UV cutoff**, **10.33 V rising UV recovery**, and **14.75 V rising OV cutoff**. RS1 is a Kelvin, 8.2 mΩ, 1% shunt. Its 40–60 mV normal-output threshold bracket gives approximately **4.83–7.39 A** before temperature and PCB errors. U1 latches after forward overcurrent. Check a short with the actual adapter and the converter operating. [LTC4368 datasheet](https://www.analog.com/media/en/technical-documentation/data-sheets/ltc4368.pdf) · [PSMN4R8-100BSE pinout and SOA](https://assets.nexperia.com/documents/data-sheet/PSMN4R8-100BSE.pdf)
- **Adapter.** At 3 A output the load needs about **34.5 W**. At 90% assumed end-to-end efficiency the input draws **3.19 A at 12.0 V** or **3.36 A at 11.4 V** as measured at J1. Check measured voltage and current during inrush and sustained load. Verify the Rockchip connector’s actual JST family, contact rating, and polarity. Both retrofit headers are JST VH B2P-VH(LF)(SN), with pin 1 positive and pin 2 return. [JST VH catalog](https://www.jst-mfg.com/product/pdf/eng/eVH.pdf)

## Regulator and cutoff

| Circuit | Design intent and values |
| --- | --- |
| **U2, Q3–Q6, L1** | LTC3789EGN in SSOP-28 drives four 60 V CSD18540Q5B MOSFETs around the 4.7 µH Coilcraft XAL1010-472MED. Q3/Q4 form the input leg and Q5/Q6 the output leg. RS2 provides bidirectional inductor-current sensing at the shared low-side source return. At 15 mΩ its datasheet boost peak threshold range corresponds to about 8.2–10.5 A before shunt tolerance, so check the inductor and MOSFETs during startup and a short. Compensation uses an initial 14.7 kΩ / 10 nF series branch and a 1 nF shunt. Verify loop stability in buck, boost, and transition using the selected inductor and effective capacitance. [LTC3789 datasheet](https://www.analog.com/media/en/technical-documentation/data-sheets/3789fc.pdf) · [DC1523A reference schematic](https://www.analog.com/media/en/technical-documentation/eval-board-schematic/1523asch.pdf) · [Coilcraft inductor](https://www.coilcraft.com/en-us/products/power/shielded-inductors/molded-inductor/xal/xal1010/xal1010-472/) · [TI MOSFET pinout](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/csd18540q5b.pdf) |
| **Input current regulation** | RS3 is 12 mΩ, 1%, between the input bulk capacitor and switching input ceramics. The 48–52.5 mV average-current specification gives about **3.96–4.42 A** before sense-filter and temperature error. R17 and R18 compensate the typical input bias mismatch. This loop targets the adapter’s sustained current. U1 covers wiring and pass-switch faults. [LTC3789 current-sense design, Figure 8](https://www.analog.com/media/en/technical-documentation/data-sheets/3789fc.pdf#page=16) |
| **Voltage feedback and filtering** | R7–R9 give `0.800 × (1 + (13.3 kΩ + 75 Ω) / 1 kΩ) = 11.50 V` nominal. The 0.788–0.812 V full-range reference and 0.1% initial divider tolerances imply approximately **11.31–11.69 V DC** at VREG before temperature drift, line and load regulation, and ripple. C19 is a 330 µF, 35 V Panasonic hybrid. The 50 V ceramics at VBUS_SW and VREG are TDK C3225X7R1H226M250AC. Check their effective capacitance under DC bias. Allow for switch and harness drop at J2. [TDK ceramic capacitor](https://product.tdk.com/en/search/capacitor/ceramic/mlcc/info?part_no=C3225X7R1H226M250AC) · [Panasonic hybrid capacitor](https://industrial.panasonic.com/ww/products/pt/hybrid-aluminum/models?op=search&order=atr-ind-pRDD0000J1_3_A001&series=ABA0141+ABA0161&sort=asc) |
| **Independent trip** | U3 generates 5 V bias from VPROT_IN, upstream of the converter. Its delayed PG and U5’s open-drain OV_OK feed U6. U4 provides the independent 2.5 V reference. U5 latches low when the sampled VREG rises above it. R23 and R24 set **11.80 V nominal** and D7 limits the comparator sense input during a sustained fault. U6 drops PATH_EN to disable U2’s RUN input and pull down the output switch through U7. Check for unwanted turn-on with VREG already high at startup, during brownout, and when clearing the latch. TP5 momentarily connects CLR to return. Bias removal also resets the latch. [TPS7A1650 datasheet](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tps7a16.pdf) · [REF5025A datasheet](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/ref50.pdf) · [TLV9020L1 datasheet](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tlv9030l.pdf?ts=1794342925919) · [SN74LVC1G08 pinout](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/sn74lvc1g08.pdf) |
| **Output switch** | U7 is an LTC7001EMSE with Q7/Q8 in a common-source pair that blocks both directions when off. C28 supports both gates, and D6 precharges it. R31 shapes turn-on while TGDN connects directly for turn-off. R32 is 2.2 MΩ to keep its steady load within the bootstrap pump’s current budget. Measure the actual gate-source waveform, including initial turn-on, hot disconnect, and loss of bias. Check D9 and U3 OUT-to-IN stress if the input rail collapses faster than its bias output. [LTC7001 datasheet](https://www.analog.com/media/en/technical-documentation/data-sheets/ltc7001.pdf) |

The 11.80 V nominal trip leaves **0.20 V** to cover trip tolerance, response time, overshoot, and measurement uncertainty. The highest normal VREG peak must stay below the lowest trip threshold. The highest transient at J2 plus measurement uncertainty must stay at or below **12.0 V**. Calculate the final trip corners from the chosen passives and verify both limits on hardware, especially during fast fault transitions.

## Layout and checks

Use four layers as a starting stackup, with the switching components and wide power copper on L1, a continuous return plane on L2, local power on L3, and ground plus low-speed routing on L4. Place D1 at J1, place ceramics at the switch legs, keep the two switch nodes small, and keep the output monitor out of their electric field. Route the dedicated shunt sense pairs from the inner lands. Join SGND to GND at R12 next to the input capacitor’s quiet return. Match the diode stripe, capacitor polarity, MOSFET drain land, and exposed-pad geometry to the exact package drawings. [TI four-switch layout guidance](https://www.ti.com/document-viewer/lit/html/SSZTAE5/GUID-1402DA8B-73DB-44B4-B203-A3AF307A6C45)

| Check | Acceptance |

| EasyEDA | Import the A3 Standard JSON into an open EasyEDA Pro project. Verify the embedded devices, choose and confirm footprint land patterns, and run its electrical checks. PCB placement, routing, DRC, and manufacturing artwork are pending. [EasyEDA Pro import](https://prodocs.easyeda.com/en/faq/import-export/) |
| Normal boot and shutdown | First use a dummy load across idle to 3 A. Use the actual adapter and harness and confirm no startup hiccup, excessive voltage drop, or overshoot. Confirm the Rockchip cold-boots and runs at the actual J2 voltage. |
| Hardware cutoff | With a dummy load, measure the static trip and inject a feedback open, an overvoltage already present at startup, and fast rising faults. Probe VREG, J2, PATH_EN, and OUT_GATE relative to OUT_SOURCE. Verify that both shutdown actions occur, the output stays within its 12.0 V ceiling, and reset behavior matches the machine’s needs. |
| Installed immunity | Qualify ESD, EFT, and surge at the applicable ports in the intended cabinet with the harness and grounding installed. Test other accessible Rockchip ports separately. [IEC ESD](https://webstore.iec.ch/en/publication/68954) · [IEC EFT](https://webstore.iec.ch/en/publication/4222) · [IEC surge](https://webstore.iec.ch/en/publication/61166) |
