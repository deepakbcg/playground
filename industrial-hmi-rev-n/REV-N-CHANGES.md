## Inductor review basis

- **L1** feeds the internal 1.1 V RP2354B core. Use a preliminary 0.30 A DC allowance and the official Raspberry Pi minimal-design component and electrical curves. Check saturation at the regulator’s peak current and verify operating current with representative firmware. [RP2350 resources](https://pip.raspberrypi.com/categories/1214-rp2350)
- **L2** supplies the 133 mA backlight at approximately 10.5 V. The 80% boost assumption implies 0.35 A nominal at 5 V and 0.39 A at 4.5 V. Keep headroom for inductor ripple, current limit and temperature. The original Bourns sheet rates it at 2.75 A RMS and 3.5 A saturation. [SRP5030TA datasheet](https://www.bourns.com/docs/product-datasheets/srp5030ta.pdf)
- **L3** uses a 1.8 A steady 5 V design allowance. With 10 µH, 400 kHz and 27.6 V input, ideal ripple is about 1.02 A p-p and nominal peak is about 2.31 A. Competent fault margin must also address the regulator’s 6.0 A maximum specified peak-limit value, plus temperature and inductance tolerance. [LMR51635 datasheet](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/lmr51635.pdf) · [SRP1038A datasheet](https://www.bourns.com/docs/product-datasheets/srp1038a.pdf)
- **L4** uses a 1.1 A allocated continuous 3.3 V load. At 5 V input, 2.5 MHz and 1 µH, ideal ripple is about 0.45 A p-p and nominal peak is about 1.33 A. Qualification must include the 2 A rated rail and current-limit behavior. The exact Sunlord part is retained. [TPS62902 datasheet](https://www.ti.com/lit/ds/symlink/tps62902.pdf) · [Sunlord listing](https://jlcpcb.com/partdetail/C408332)
