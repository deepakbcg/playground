# Standards source notes

Checked 2026-11-24. Select standards and revisions for the actual product, contract and market. These notes describe scope. Use the applicable publication to obtain requirements or numerical limits.

## Conductor current and heating

IPC-2152 addresses how conductor size, current and permitted temperature rise relate on printed boards. Use a suitable sizing method that accounts for actual copper construction and thermal environment. The resistance example in the local rulebook estimates copper loss and voltage drop. Temperature rise needs a separate evaluation. [IPC-2152 public scope](https://www.ipc.org/TOC/IPC-2152.pdf)

## Insulation coordination

IEC 60664-1 provides basic insulation-coordination requirements within its defined scope. Identify the applicable product standard and operating conditions first. Derive clearance, creepage and solid insulation requirements separately. Record inputs such as working voltage, transient conditions, pollution degree, material group and altitude where required. The publisher lists a 2025 amendment and a consolidated edition. [IEC 60664-1 publication record](https://webstore.iec.ch/en/publication/59671)
